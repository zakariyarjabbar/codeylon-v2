# Codeylon

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User specified Next.js, TypeScript, and the App Router. Static export; no backend or paid-service dependencies.

## Users

Businesses, independent professionals, creators, and organisations seeking an informational website that explains an offer, showcases work, and makes contact straightforward.

## Product Purpose

Help suitable prospective customers understand Codeylon's offer, assess its design quality, and start a website conversation on Instagram.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Business and service websites, landing pages, portfolios, restaurant websites and menus, informational product showcases, and redesigns. No accounts, checkout, dashboards, databases, or complex backends. No public prices, invented proof, unsupported promises, tracking, or invented contact channels.

## Brand Commitments

Codeylon; https://codeylon.com; Instagram https://www.instagram.com/codeylon/. Preserve supplied SVG geometry. Navy #091A44 and gold #BD9A41; neo-brutalism and immediately visible pixel art. Direct natural English.

## Evidence on Hand

Supplied Group4444.svg and Group33.png. No real client work or testimonials provided. Three fictional concept projects must be visibly labelled.

## Product Principles

Clear offer before decoration. Honest examples before unsubstantiated claims. Easy contact without forms or backend. Inclusive mobile and keyboard use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable type, contrast, semantic structure, visible focus, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, responsive layouts, and useful image alternatives.
